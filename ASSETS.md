# Asset provenance and redistribution

On November 18, 2026, the project owner authorized inclusion of the current Arviva assets in the public vickyteke/cedari-agent-template repository. This covers the Arviva logo/launcher artwork and inspirational onboarding photograph. The template code is MIT licensed; inclusion of artwork does not grant ownership of Arviva trademarks or imply endorsement of derivative apps. Operators should replace branding before publishing their own apps.

- apps/mobile/assets/icon.png, arviva-logo.svg, Android icon variants, favicon.png and splash-icon.png are the approved source Arviva branding.
- apps/mobile/assets/onboarding/warm-living-room.jpg is the approved Canva-generated inspirational interior photograph.
- apps/mobile/assets/play-feature-graphic.png is approved source store artwork; replace it for derivative store listings.
- apps/backend/tests/fixtures/room-reference.jpg was visually reviewed: it is a synthetic illustrated room, with no customer photograph or identifying information.
- SVG UI icon paths and system font names remain in source. No commercial font files are bundled. Preserve applicable third-party notices.

The publication check requires ASSET_RIGHTS_CONFIRMED=true as an explicit acknowledgement of this review. Fork maintainers must review the rights to assets they add or replace.
